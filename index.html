Exam rules at a glance

Important differences between the four papers.
IE1030 • DCN

Computer Based

  • All 9 lectures + tutorials + practicals
  • 10 MCQs (20 marks)
  • 4 structured short answers (30 marks)
  • 10 essay-type questions (50 marks)
  • Referral sheet allowed; physical calculator not allowed
IT1120 • IP

Computer Based / NetExam

  • 25 MCQs = 50%
  • 2 Java programs = 50%
  • 2 hours + 10 min upload time
  • .java files must be uploaded
  • No Internet; restricted desktop tools only
IT1130 • MC

Written

  • 4 structured questions
  • Logic Control
  • Partial Fractions + Trig + Complex
  • Differentiation + Integration
  • Matrices
  • Standard physical calculator only; no scientific calculator
IT1140 • FC

Written

  • 4 essay-type questions
  • Lectures 2–9
  • Lecture 1 & 10 excluded
  • Referral sheet allowed
  • Calculators not allowed
